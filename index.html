Before you go out

This is a horror game. It has a monster that hunts you, jump scares, children in danger, and graphic, bloody deaths.

Gore is set to Full: you'll see what he leaves behind. If you'd rather not, Settings → Gore → "Quick cut" cuts to black at the moment it happens.

Play it in the dark, with headphones. The walkie-talkie hisses when he is close.

TRICKORTREAT

Toby dropped his candy as he was taken. Follow it. You can't fight what took him: stay low, stay out of the light, and listen. Your walkie-talkie picks him up when he's near.

If he sees you, run, break his line of sight, then hide. If he watches you hide, he knows where you are. The neighbours on their porches will scream for him if they see you.

Some of the Lane's old jack-o'-lanterns have names carved in them. Blow them out.
